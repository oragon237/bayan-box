## What You Sell
- **To merchants**: sell on HABI (90/10 split, HABI Mall, ads, Suki Points loyalty, affiliate program, merchant dashboards/reports/payouts)
- **To riders**: earn via delivery (85/15 split), round-robin assignments, wallet + cash-outs
- **To providers**: skilled-worker marketplace, verified badges, bookings, payouts
- **To hubs/staff**: logistics OS, dispatch, inventory, finance

## Key Selling Points (ground in real features)
- 90/10 merchant/platform split on regular sales; 100% admin earnings on HABI Mall
- Affiliate program with referral codes + QR and ID-verified cash-outs (min ₱200)
- Escrow-led financial integrity: orders reconcile 100% to ledger, zero negative balances
- Delivery fees: ₱40 base + ₱10/km (dynamic, OSRM road distance)
- PWA works offline in low-connectivity provincial areas
